THE HOLLOW LOOP

PROTOTYPE · REGION 1

Leave the hub, push through the hall, the corridor and the cistern, defeat the Gatekeeper, and take the barred passage home. That passage only opens from the far side.

Your blade swings on its own at anything inside its reach arc. Red shapes on the floor are incoming attacks: dash out before they fill. You can't be hurt mid-dash. Strike foes while they recover for critical hits.

Altars offer boons that change how you fight. They last until you rest or fall.

At a crystal you can Rest (restore health, but the dungeon resets and your boons fade) or Save (bank carried embers and set your return point, keeping your boons). If you die, you lose carried embers and boons and wake at your last save.
